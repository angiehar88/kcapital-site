Advisory Services

From a financeable project to committed capital

K Capital can support the full debt financing journey or a defined stage, from shaping the project around financing requirements to securing and closing debt.

01

Build a financeable project

Build lender requirements into the project early

We start with the questions a lender will ask: where does repayment come from, what risks sit with the project and what could prevent credit approval? We review the economics, commercial logic, counterparties, contracts, permits and risk allocation to identify the issues that materially affect bankability.

Where gaps can be addressed, we work with the client to refine the structure, assess key participants, shape principal commercial terms and align the contractual and financing framework with lender requirements.

What this gives the client

  • An early view of the issues that materially affect debt capacity or credit approval.
  • A prioritised plan to improve financeability and focus development effort where it matters most.
  • A stronger project structure, risk allocation and set of key commercial terms.
  • A clearer basis for informed investment, project development and PPP bid decisions.

02

Define the financing route

Identify financing routes that fit the project

Once the project logic is clear, we identify financing routes that fit its country, sector, size, procurement, cash flow profile, sponsor strength and stage of development. We then test the eligibility, credit requirements and practical constraints of the relevant sources and reflect those requirements in the project and financing strategy.

What this gives the client

  • A focused view of the funding sources and structures worth pursuing, with the rationale for each.
  • Clarity on what needs to be addressed before approaching the relevant institutions.
  • An indicative financing concept aligned with the project and likely lender requirements.
  • A financing roadmap with the sequence of decisions, preparation and market engagement.

03

Prepare for lender engagement

Build the credit case and define the negotiating position

Lenders need more than a presentation. We organise the transaction around how banks, ECAs and DFIs will assess it while keeping the client's commercial objectives explicit. Before outreach, the financing objectives, preferred terms and areas of flexibility should be clearly defined, together with the information lenders will need to reach a credit decision.

Preparation can include a teaser, information memorandum, lender presentation, financial model and sensitivities, review of key project contracts and risk allocation, closure of material information gaps, anticipated lender Q&A and a clear negotiation agenda.

What this gives the client

  • A coherent financing case that lenders can assess efficiently.
  • A clear negotiating position, including financing objectives and key points to protect.
  • Project materials organised around the issues most likely to drive credit approval.

04

Raise financing and reach financial close

Move from lender engagement to committed financing

We select and approach relevant institutions, test appetite confidentially where appropriate, manage market feedback and keep the process focused. As terms emerge, we support negotiations and coordinate the financing workstream through lender due diligence, documentation, conditions precedent and financial close.

What this gives the client

  • A targeted lender universe and disciplined market approach.
  • A clear view of lender feedback and how the financing route should respond.
  • Support on term sheets, including pricing, tenor, repayment, security and covenants.
  • Hands-on support through lender due diligence, including information requests, Q&A and coordination across the client team and advisers.
  • Senior coordination of the financing process through financial close.

Financing structures and sources

  • Project finance
  • Structured finance
  • ECA-backed finance
  • DFI financing
  • Corporate and syndicated debt
  • Asset-based finance
  • Bridge finance
  • Sovereign-backed and government-supported financing

Special situations

K Capital also advises on selected acquisition financings, capital structures, distressed or restructuring situations and asset-based transactions. We also support sponsors and investors evaluating investment opportunities and PPP tenders where financeability needs to be understood before capital is committed or a bid is submitted.

Ongoing financing partner

For clients with a recurring pipeline of investments and debt financings, K Capital can work alongside the internal team on a flexible basis, for a defined period or across an ongoing programme. We help interpret the financing landscape, screen opportunities, shape funding strategies, strengthen the client's position in negotiations with lenders and other counterparties, and support implementation across projects and transactions. This can suit sponsors, developers, corporates, investors, EPC contractors and equipment suppliers that need senior financing capability on an ongoing or as-needed basis.

We can join at any stage

Earlier involvement creates more room to shape the project around financing requirements. Where lenders are already engaged, we can step into a live process and focus on the decisions, negotiations and coordination needed to move it forward.
