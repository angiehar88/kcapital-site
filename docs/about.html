About K Capital

Independent debt advisory grounded in lender and transaction experience

Capital is rarely the constraint. Bankability usually is

A strong commercial case does not automatically make a project financeable. Lenders, ECAs and DFIs look at the same transaction through different mandates, credit frameworks, priorities and constraints. A financing strategy needs to work across those perspectives.

I have spent more than twenty years on different sides of that process: inside commercial and development banking, at a national export credit agency, as a financial adviser and as a mandated lead arranger.

That combination matters because it allows me to look at a project from more than one angle.

I understand how lenders analyse risk, build a credit case and take decisions through internal approval processes. I also bring first-hand export credit experience and extensive transaction experience working with ECAs, policy banks and development finance institutions, with an understanding of how their mandates, eligibility criteria, country and sector priorities, risk appetite and institutional constraints shape financing decisions.

On the client side, that translates into a practical advantage: anticipating the questions different institutions are likely to ask, identifying issues before they become obstacles, distinguishing financing routes that are realistically available from those that are not, and shaping the project and financing strategy accordingly.

My advisory and arranging experience adds the execution perspective: how to position a transaction, approach institutions in the right sequence, interpret their feedback, reconcile different requirements and keep the financing process moving towards approval and close.

I founded K Capital to help clients turn complex projects into credible, financeable transactions and carry them through to execution.

K Capital is an independent adviser focused on debt financing for complex projects, assets and transactions. We help clients determine:

  • whether a project is financeable in its current form;
  • which financing routes are genuinely realistic;
  • what needs to change before approaching the market; and
  • how to structure and execute the financing process.

We can become involved early, while there is still room to shape the project around financing requirements, or step into a live process where lenders are already engaged.

K Capital is deliberately senior-led. I remain directly involved throughout each mandate, bringing in specialist expertise where the transaction requires it.

The objective is to see the transaction as financing institutions will see it, before they do, and use that perspective to improve the client's path to financing.
