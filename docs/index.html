Build financing requirements into the project early

A sound commercial case does not by itself make a project financeable. Lenders assess how cash flows, risks, contracts, counterparties and execution fit together. Testing these issues early gives the client more options to address gaps before the financing process is constrained by decisions already made.

K Capital brings experience from commercial and development banking, export credit agency, corporate finance advisory and debt arranging to the client side. We use that perspective to anticipate credit questions, strengthen the financing case and stay close to the transaction through execution.

What we help clients achieve

  1. 01

    Build a financeable project

    Test project economics and risk allocation from a creditor perspective, identify material obstacles early and strengthen the project structure, counterparties and key commercial terms needed to support debt.

  2. 02

    Find the right financing route

    Identify realistic funding sources and structures based on the project, country, sector, procurement, cash flows and sponsor profile, then align the financing strategy with the requirements of the most relevant routes.

  3. 03

    Prepare for lender engagement

    Present a coherent credit case and a clear sponsor position, supported by the model, project materials and negotiation priorities needed for informed lender discussions.

  4. 04

    Raise capital and reach financial close

    Approach the right institutions, test appetite, interpret feedback, negotiate terms and coordinate lender due diligence, documentation and conditions through financial close.
