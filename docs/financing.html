Financing Solutions

Financing solutions matched to the project

Complex projects may have several potential financing sources and structures, which can be combined or sequenced as the project develops. K Capital assesses the project, jurisdiction, sector, procurement, cash flows, sponsor profile and risk allocation to identify the routes that are genuinely relevant and build them into an executable funding plan.

Project and structured finance

For projects where repayment is primarily linked to project cash flows, contracts and risk allocation, we help develop a financeable structure, identify suitable lenders and coordinate the financing process. Where conventional project finance is not the best fit, we consider structured debt solutions tailored to the transaction's specific cash flows, assets and support package.

ECA-backed finance

For sponsors and investors, export credit support can be a material part of the financing where procurement origin, country eligibility and agency appetite align. We assess potential ECA routes early, help shape sourcing and financing structures, identify relevant banks and agencies and align project and commercial documentation with financing requirements.

For EPC contractors, exporters and equipment suppliers, the same capability can help build a financing route around the commercial offer and strengthen the buyer's ability to fund the contract.

DFI financing

Development finance institutions and development banks can be relevant where the project, country and development mandate align. We help identify the institutions that fit the transaction, understand their credit and eligibility requirements, prepare the project for engagement and integrate their participation with other debt sources where appropriate.

Corporate and bridge debt

Where financing is supported by the sponsor or operating business rather than solely by project cash flows, the route may involve bilateral corporate debt. Bridge financing can also be used where timing requires an interim solution before longer-term funding is available.

Syndicated financing

Where several lenders participate in the same financing, the debt can be structured on a syndicated basis at project or corporate level. We help define the lender group and financing structure, coordinate market engagement and support negotiations and execution across the syndicate.

Asset-based and procurement-linked finance

For transactions where a specific asset, procurement package or export contract is central to credit, financing can be structured around the asset, its cash-generating capacity and the underlying commercial arrangements. Depending on the asset and jurisdiction, this may include secured lending, leasing or other asset-based structures. K Capital's experience includes aircraft and shipping finance as well as export-backed structures.

Sovereign-backed and government-supported finance

For public-sector and strategic projects, the financing route may involve sovereign or quasi-sovereign borrowing, guarantees, government-supported programmes or government-to-government frameworks. We help assess how public support fits the credit structure and how it can be combined with commercial, ECA or development finance.

The right solution is often a combination of sources

The objective is to identify a financing route that is financeable, executable and aligned with the client's commercial objectives, then carry it through to lender engagement and financial close.
